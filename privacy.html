Before activating real enquiries or payment processing, Aerie must publish a complete privacy notice identifying the operator, contact details, processing purposes and lawful basis, processors, retention periods, customer rights and safeguards.

Any hosting platform may collect technical access logs according to its own policies. No analytics or tracking scripts are included in this demo.
