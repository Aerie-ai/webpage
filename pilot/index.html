    04 / Where next

    Why this lab is different

    Local processing is the default. We intend to use a genuine AI model only when it clearly improves a task, with a transparent choice of local or properly secured hosted processing. No model is connected in this version. Future safeguards will need authenticated encrypted storage, audited approvals and independent security review before real business use.
