How Aerie is different

Useful work without surrendering your data

All six tools use local arithmetic, structured questions and transparent decision rules. They are prototypes and are not connected to email, payment processors, calendars, energy meters, company databases or AI models. The CSV exports are for fictional examples only.

The next challenge is not adding a costly general-purpose AI everywhere; it is testing which real workflows can be reliably improved, then building permissioned integrations, independently reviewed storage and actual energy measurements where necessary.
